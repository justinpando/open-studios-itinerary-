# Open Studios Planner

Pick artists from the Portland Open Studios 2026 directory, see their work, and get the shortest driving order between their studios, with Google Maps directions for each day.

- Start from your current location, an address, a studio you're at, or a spot on the map.
- Drive times between studios are real road times (OpenStreetMap / OSRM, no traffic).
- Your picks are saved in your browser.

Artist names, addresses and images come from portlandopenstudios.com. Map © OpenStreetMap contributors.
